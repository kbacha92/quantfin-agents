ACME CORPORATION ANNUAL REPORT (FORM 10-K) — Fiscal Year 2024. Filed 2024-11-01.
Item 1A. Risk Factors. The company faces concentration risk: its largest customer
accounted for 38 percent of fiscal 2024 revenue. A loss of this customer would
materially harm results. Supply chain risk: ACME sources 90 percent of its
specialty resin from a single supplier in Region B. Item 7. Management Discussion.
Fiscal 2024 revenue was 4.20 billion dollars, up 6 percent year over year. Net
income was 512 million dollars. The board authorized a 300 million dollar buyback.
