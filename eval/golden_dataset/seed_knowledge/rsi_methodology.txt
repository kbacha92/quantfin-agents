Technical Analysis Reference: Relative Strength Index (RSI). The RSI is a momentum
oscillator measuring the speed and magnitude of price changes on a 0 to 100 scale.
It is computed from average gains and average losses over a lookback window, most
commonly 14 periods, using Wilder's smoothing. Readings above 70 are conventionally
considered overbought and readings below 30 oversold. RSI is a methodology, not a
statement about any specific security on any specific date.
