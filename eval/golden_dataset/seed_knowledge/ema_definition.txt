Technical Analysis Reference: Exponential Moving Average (EMA). The exponential
moving average is a type of moving average that places greater weight on the most
recent prices, making it more responsive to new information than a simple moving
average. It is computed by applying a smoothing factor to the current price and
the prior EMA value. Conceptually, EMA reduces lag relative to a simple moving
average of the same window. This is a methodology definition and is timeless; it
does not describe any specific security on any specific date.
